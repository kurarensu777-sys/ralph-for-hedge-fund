# Research - the shelf

Kept by the research agent. `research.md` is the guide: how a study is done here. A strategy
book (`type: strategy`, `parent: research`) holds what the fund knows about one trading topic,
and its learnings are its chapters; none ships yet, and a desk's first lesson starts one. What a
desk retro says travels up lands here as a `Lesson:` chapter; the desk's `B<n>` beliefs stay in
its lab ledger (`platform/lab/<desk>/beliefs.md`).

| Book | Read when |
|---|---|
| `research.md` | about to start, plan, name, label, pin, wrap or summarise a study, draw its figures to the house style, or plan one that trades many times a day (the notebook in studies/ is the deliverable, never a draft board) |
| `notebook-craft.md` | about to write a notebook cell or its question headers, write a next bet, rewrite a notebook on a review board, guard a vendor series, or say a backtest is verified |
| `charts.md` | about to draw a chart, add a chart type, or tag a figure's type in a manifest (the guide and the type list) |
| `charts-describe.md` | about to draw a series, an equity curve, drawdowns, signal decay, calibration, rolling statistics or ruin odds |
| `charts-test.md` | about to draw an out-of-sample test, a luck test, sample size, a parameter surface, a rule comparison or a factor regression |
| `charts-trade.md` | about to draw costs, capacity, size and fill, a hedge, regimes, fund-book correlation or a scenario payoff |
