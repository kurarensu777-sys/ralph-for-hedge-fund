# Academic references - the shelf

What the textbooks and papers say, boiled to two levels: the high-level claim, then the equations an
agent needs to apply it, with the symbols defined. An agent opens a book here when it builds or
judges a strategy and the question is already answered in the literature. This shelf holds outside
sources only; what OUR runs found lands in `app/library/research/`.

Each chapter names its source (author, year, venue) and opens with its Read-when line. A formula
is recorded from the cited paper and tagged `[U]` until a second hand has checked it against the
paper's own text; the book's header carries the tag. A new book is one source family (a book or a
paper cluster) and its learnings are `##` chapters.
